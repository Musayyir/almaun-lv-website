Youth Conference

For the next generation of the Westside.

A free, all-day gathering built for the young people of the Historic Westside — hands-on workshops, real mentorship, speakers worth hearing, good food, and a whole lot of fun. Bring a friend. Bring the whole family.

Next conference: date announced soon — reserve your spot to be notified.

About Youth Development

What to expect

A full day, made for them.

No lectures-only, no talking down. A warm, hands-on day where young people learn something real, meet people who believe in them, and actually have fun doing it.

Hands-on workshops

Small, practical sessions — from goal-setting and study skills to money basics, creativity, and standing tall in who you are.

Real mentorship

Time with mentors from the neighborhood who've walked the road — honest conversations, not a script. Everyone leaves knowing someone in their corner.

Speakers worth hearing

Voices young people can see themselves in — elders, leaders, and doers from the Westside and beyond, sharing what actually helped them.

Good food, on us

Breakfast and a hot lunch, provided — because nobody learns hungry, and a shared meal is where the block becomes family.

Games & fun

Chess, activities, teams, prizes, and plenty of room to just be a kid. Serious growth, wrapped in a genuinely good time.

New friends

Leave with a wider circle — peers who are building something, and a community that keeps showing up long after the day ends.

Who it's for

The young people of the block — and their families.

The Youth Conference is for kids and teens across the Historic Westside, and the parents and grandparents who love them. Whether your family comes every Friday or has never set foot in the masjid, you belong here. Muslim or not, no cost, no paperwork, no judgment — just neighbors investing in neighbors.

Day-of

How the day flows.

Here's the shape of a typical conference day. Times and sessions are a sample to give you a feel — the final schedule is confirmed closer to the date.

Sample schedule · subject to change

9:30 AM

Doors open & breakfast

Check in, grab a bite, and settle in with new and familiar faces.

10:00 AM

Welcome & opening

A warm start together — who we are, why we gather, and what the day holds.

10:30 AM

Workshop rotations

Small hands-on sessions in groups — pick what speaks to you.

12:00 PM

Lunch, provided

A hot meal and a real break — food, games, and good company.

1:00 PM

Keynote & speakers

Voices from the community sharing stories, wisdom, and encouragement.

2:00 PM

Mentorship circles & activities

Smaller-group time with mentors, plus teams, chess, and challenges.

3:00 PM

Closing & du'a

We wrap up together, celebrate the day, and send everyone off with prizes.

Zakat & Sadaqah

Yes — this is zakat-eligible.

Feeding the poor, helping the needy, and supporting the wayfarer are named in the Qur'an as the very purposes of zakat. That is precisely the work Al-Maun does, every week, on the Westside — so your zakat can be given here with confidence.

إِنَّمَا الصَّدَقَاتُ لِلْفُقَرَاءِ وَالْمَسَاكِينِ

“Charity is only for the poor and the needy…”

Qur'an · Surah At-Tawbah 9:60

  1. 1Decide what you owe. Use the quick calculator — zakat is 2.5% of the wealth you've held for a full lunar year, once it's above nisab.
  2. 2In the donation form, set “Designate this gift” to Zakat (or leave it on Sadaqah for general charity). Your choice is noted with the gift.
  3. 3Give — and know it flows straight to eligible neighbors: meals, essentials, and a hand up for the needy and the wayfarer.

Trust & transparency

Give with a clear conscience.

Al-Maun Neighborly Needs is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit — so your gift is tax-deductible, and you'll have the paperwork to prove it. We keep our promises small and our books honest.

A gentle tip: where you're given the option, choose a fee-free processor — or add the small fee to your gift — so that close to 100% of what you give reaches the work instead of a payment company.

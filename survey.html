Tell us what the Westside needs next.

A handful of quick questions — under two minutes. Your answers guide which programs we grow, and they become the evidence we bring to grant-makers and donors so Al-Maun can keep showing up for our neighbors.

1 Which best describes you?
2 What part of the valley are you in?
3 Which of our programs have you or your family used? Check all that apply — or “None yet.”
4 How much has Al-Maun helped you or your household?
5 What does our community need most right now? Pick the two or three that matter most to you.
6 How did you first hear about Al-Maun?
7 Anything else you’d like us to know? Optional — a sentence or two is perfect.

Want us to follow up? (optional)

Leave your name and email only if you’d like a reply. You can answer completely anonymously.

Why we ask

The best way to know what our neighbors need is to ask them. Your answers help us decide what to serve, when, and where — from food and health to youth and reentry.

They also give us something powerful: real evidence, straight from the community, that we bring to grant applications and donors so the work can grow.

Our story

A name that means small kindnesses.

Al-Ma'un is the 107th chapter of the Qur'an — a short, piercing surah that measures faith not by grand gestures, but by whether you feed the orphan and help your neighbor. That is our whole job description.

We were born inside Masjid As-Sabur, the oldest mosque in Las Vegas, rooted in the Historic Westside since long before the neighborhood had many friends. From that little corner on Morgan Avenue, volunteers started showing up for whoever knocked — the hungry, the homeless, seniors, veterans, returning citizens, kids. That's still exactly how it works today.

Along the way the work got noticed: nationally recognized relief after Hurricane Katrina, and a visit from Muhammad Ali himself. But the point was never the recognition. The point is the neighbor in front of you, right now.

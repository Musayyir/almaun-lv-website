Donate goods

Drop off exactly what a neighbor needs.

The everyday stuff moves fast around here. If you can spare any of the items below — new or gently used where noted — bring them by and they'll be in a neighbor's hands within days.

This week's needs

  • Water
  • Cooling fans
  • Hygiene kits
  • Diapers
  • Baby formula
  • Backpacks
  • New socks & underwear
  • Non-perishable food
  • Gently-used jackets

Not sure if we can use something? Bring it by anyway — if we can't place it, we'll point it somewhere that can.

Where to drop off

711 Morgan Ave
Las Vegas, NV 89106

Drop off during our open hours. Please call ahead at (702) 647-2500 so a volunteer can meet you and help unload.

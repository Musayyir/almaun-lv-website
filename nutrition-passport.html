How it works

Like a passport — stamp by stamp.

Managing your health shouldn't feel like homework. The Nutrition Passport turns it into small, doable wins. You show up, you try one thing, you pass it on — and you collect your “stamps” along the way.

  1. 1Learn — come to a free workshop and pick up one simple idea. A hot meal is provided, so bring the family.
  2. 2Do — try it at home. Track a habit, cook a recipe, take a short walk. Small steps, real progress.
  3. 3Share — tell a neighbor what worked, or bring a friend to the next workshop. Kindness travels.

The program

Three levels, at your pace.

Start wherever you are. Each level is a little step forward — and there's no wrong place to begin.

1

Level 1 · Learn

Come to a workshop

The fundamentals, made friendly.

  • Reading food labels without the guesswork
  • Portions & smart swaps for everyday meals
  • Spotting hidden sugar — and cutting back
  • Simple, affordable cooking you'll actually use
  • A hot meal is provided at every workshop
2

Level 2 · Do

Put it into practice

Turn one idea into a habit.

  • Track a simple habit, one day at a time
  • Try a new recipe from the workshop
  • Finish small health activities & challenges
  • Move a little more — a walk counts
  • Celebrate the wins, however small
Practice at the next workshop
3

Level 3 · Share

Pass it on

Health spreads best neighbor-to-neighbor.

  • Share what you learned with family
  • Cook a healthier meal for someone you love
  • Bring a friend or neighbor to a workshop
  • Encourage each other to keep going
  • Become a familiar, friendly face in the room

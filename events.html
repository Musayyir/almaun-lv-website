Annual flagship

Humanitarian Day

Once a year · spring — the exact date is announced each winter

Masjid As-Sabur · 711 Morgan Ave, Las Vegas

Our single biggest day of service. In one recent afternoon we welcomed 500+ neighbors and gave out more than $20,000 in direct aid — free, with dignity, no questions asked. On the day you'll find:

  • Hot meals, water, hygiene kits and mobile showers
  • Free medical services, haircuts and eye care
  • Shoes, jackets, backpacks and cooling fans
  • Baby formula, diapers and family essentials

RSVP — volunteer or attend

Food & Nutrition

Weekly Food Pantry

Every Saturday · 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM

Masjid As-Sabur · 711 Morgan Ave, Las Vegas

Every Saturday morning we set out groceries, fresh produce and a donated meal for anyone who needs one. No ID, no paperwork, no cost. Homebound? Ask us about grocery delivery.

  • Fresh produce, pantry staples and a donated meal to take home
  • Open to everyone — Muslim and non-Muslim, neighbor and stranger
  • Volunteers welcome from 9:00 AM to set up and serve

Youth

Youth Chess Club

Every Wednesday · 5:00 – 7:00 PM

Masjid As-Sabur · 711 Morgan Ave, Las Vegas

Part of our youth development program: boards, mentors and a snack table, every week. It's about focus, patience and belonging as much as it is about the game.

  • All skill levels — beginners get a friendly coach
  • Ages 7 to 17; younger siblings welcome with a guardian
  • Boards and snacks provided — just bring yourself

Community

Community Cleanup

First Saturday of the month · 8:30 – 11:00 AM

Meet at Masjid As-Sabur · 711 Morgan Ave

An hour or two of neighbors making the Westside shine — picking up litter, clearing lots and looking out for the block. Cool morning start before the desert heat.

  • Gloves, bags and grabbers provided
  • Great for families, student groups and first-time volunteers
  • Water and a light breakfast to send you off

Workshop

Know-Your-Rights Workshop

Last Thursday of the month · 6:00 – 8:00 PM

Masjid As-Sabur · 711 Morgan Ave, Las Vegas

Part of our education & workshops program: a free, plain-language legal-rights session led by volunteer attorneys. Ask your questions in a room that's on your side.

  • Your rights during stops, at home and at work
  • Practical steps to protect your family and documents
  • Confidential — ask anything, no records kept
